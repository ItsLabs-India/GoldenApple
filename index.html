Take the tour

A day on the Golden Apple campus, from your first hello to a bike ride round the lake.

01 · SAY HELLO

Meet your coworkers

Click anyone to see their card: role, team, status and apples collected. Poke them, wave, or say thanks.

02 · SAY THANKS

Give apples for good work

Choose a reason, add a note and send up to ten apples a day. The whole office sees it in the Orchard activity feed.

03 · GO GOLD

Become a Golden Apple

Cross ten apples and the confetti flies. You wear the golden crown and everyone gets the news.

04 · GET TO WORK

Your own desk

One of 24 desks, styled your way: monitors or a laptop, a plant, a desk pet and a couple of treasures. Only you can sit there.

05 · LUNCH

Lunch at the Pantry

A fresh menu every five minutes, from samosa and biryani to sushi and mango lassi. Sit down to eat, or brew a coffee for a boost.

06 · WORK OUT

Hit the gym

Treadmills, ellipticals, exercise bikes, weights and a punching bag. Your heart rate climbs, you sweat, and you rest up after.

07 · TAKE A BREAK

Freshen up

Private stalls with Occupied signs, showers after the gym, and sinks, because unwashed hands make lunch less healthy.

08 · PLAY

Hit the arcade

Five retro cabinets with two-player games, plus the Golden Reels slot machines on free daily credits. No real money, ever.

09 · COMPETE

Tennis and badminton

Challenge a coworker, then rally with real ball flight. First to 10, win by 2, with a scoreboard and a crowd in the stands.

10 · RIDE

Ride the Green Loop

Grab a bike from one of five stands, ring the bell and cruise round the lake. Dock it at any stand when you're done.

11 · UNWIND

Go fishing

Eight species, from tilapia to the legendary Golden Mahseer. Wait for the bite, strike, and reel without snapping the line.

Bring your team

One person opens a room and shares a 6-letter code, a link or a QR code. Everyone on the same Wi-Fi walks into the same office. No server, no password: on your own, everything stays in your browser.
